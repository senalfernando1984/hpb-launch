# Reception-Staff Operating Guide

## Access
Open the reception Web App URL provided by the organiser and sign in with your
authorised Google account. If you see "Access restricted", your account has not
been added to the allowlist — contact the administrator.

## Checking in a participant
1. **Search** by registration reference, name, NIC/passport, mobile or seat.
2. Confirm the participant's identity: ask for the original NIC or passport and
   check that it matches the masked number shown.
3. Tick **Identity document checked** once verified.
4. Enter or confirm the **seat number** and **seating category**.
5. Tick **Signature obtained** and choose the method (Physical or Digital).
6. Add a note only if necessary.
7. Tap **Mark arrived & check in**.

The system prevents a second check-in for the same participant and records every
action (who, when) in the audit log.

## If a duplicate is reported
If a participant says they registered twice, do not create another registration.
Search by their NIC/passport and check in the existing record. Duplicate
overrides are an administrator action only.

## Notes
- Identity numbers are masked by default; only the last four digits are shown.
- Do not read out or display full identity numbers.
- Exports and the full Sheet are for authorised administrators only.
